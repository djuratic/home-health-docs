Development notice. Home Health is under development. These terms describe the intended development and testing version. Mockups illustrate planned features and demonstrational data.

1. Developer

Home Health is developed by Dino Juratić, Croatia. Contact: djuratic_projects@outlook.com. Android package: hr.djuratic.homehealth.

2. Application status

Features described in application materials and mockups are planned features, not confirmation of completed integrations or successful testing. Initial use is intended for personal development and testing by an adult using their own accounts and devices.

3. Intended use

Home Health is intended to display personal health and fitness records, maintain a manual intake diary and synchronize selected data between authorized services.

It is not intended to diagnose conditions, recommend treatment, calculate medication doses or provide emergency monitoring. Medication and supplement entries record information supplied by the user. Existing ECG records and alerts, if supported, are displayed as source records rather than new medical interpretations.

4. Permissions and integrations

Connect only accounts, devices and destinations that you are authorized to use. Each integration requires the relevant permissions and user authorization.

Huawei Health → Health Connect and Health Connect → Home Assistant will be independently configurable. Availability depends on platform approval, device support, account access and implemented features.

Home Health is an independent project and is not presented as an official Huawei, Google or Home Assistant product.

5. Accuracy and availability

Records originate from connected services, devices or manual entries. They may be incomplete, delayed, duplicated or affected by differences in timestamps, units and supported data types.

The development version may contain defects or experience interruptions. Check original records when accuracy matters and maintain backups of information you need to preserve.

6. User-controlled destinations

You are responsible for choosing the correct receiving server and managing access to your Home Assistant installation, Health Data Receiver and associated backups.

Disabling an integration stops future transfers but does not automatically delete previously transferred records.

7. Privacy

The Home Health Privacy Policy explains intended processing of personal data. Acceptance of these terms does not replace consent to health-data processing or platform permission requests.

8. Ending use and changes

Features and these terms may change during development. Material changes will be communicated before they apply. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or responsibilities that cannot lawfully be excluded.
